Examples ​

These recipes model complete devices. Start with the quickstart for a small first calculation. Each recipe lists its dependencies and draws its circuit: [x] is the component named x in the code, || joins components in parallel, and node 0 is ground.

ExampleWhat it calculates
Josephson parametric amplifier (JPA)The reflection gain of a current-pumped JPA, against WRspice
JPA with a frequency dependent environmental impedanceThe same JPA through a cable from a mismatched source, the pump-off reflection against a closed form
Multi-tone Fourier gridsThe retained modes and evaluation grids of one, two and three pump tones, and their refinement
Double-pumped JPAThe gain between two pump tones, against WRspice
Flux-pumped JPAThree-wave mixing in a flux-biased SQUID, against WRspice, and the resonance against the bias
SNAIL parametric amplifierPump-on and pump-off gain near a Kerr-free point, against WRspice
Josephson traveling wave parametric amplifier (JTWPA)Gain, idlers, quantum efficiency and commutator of a resonantly phase-matched line, the pump-off line against the chain matrices of its cells
Floquet JTWPAA tapered line, lossless and with dielectric loss, the pump-off line against the chain matrices of its cells
Impedance-engineered JPAA snake amplifier built from nested subcircuits, signal and idler in power units, the pump-off reflection against a closed form
Design parameter sensitivitiesTotal and frozen-pump derivatives against finite differences, as gain derivatives
Sensitivity to frequency dependent scattering parametersThe derivative of a reflection phase against the round-trip propagation phase
Direct currentStatic flux and average voltage, a current source's sign, and a biased junction in time
Pumped finite-window noiseThe gain and quantum noise of a pumped amplifier in a finite time window
Fit a pumped amplifierA causal fit of a pumped JPA's conversion and noise, measured in time against the circuit
Pulsed Josephson lineTen pulsed signals through a Josephson line in time
Transient WRspice comparisonA JPA in time against WRspice

The stability guide works through a JPA, mode tracking across a sweep, a traveling-wave amplifier's spatial mode profile and a delay circuit, telling an operating point that converged from one that is dynamically stable. The transient guide solves a circuit in time and differentiates its response, the quantum noise guide measures the vacuum and thermal noise of a passive two-port, and the scattering guide fits scattering data and simulates the result.
